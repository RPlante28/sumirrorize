Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Sumirrorize is a smart mirror built by students at Marist University for a hackathon. It recognizes your face and shows your day: the weather, your classes, assignments, announcements and calendar. This page says what it keeps about you and why.

Sumirrorize is a student project. It isn't run or endorsed by Marist University, D2L (Brightspace) or Google.

What we collect

  • Your Google account. When you sign in we get Google's ID number for your account and your first name. We don't ask for or keep your email address.
  • Your face. The pictures you take when you sign up, and a "fingerprint" (a list of 128 numbers) made from each one. The mirror compares what its camera sees with these fingerprints to recognize you. This is biometric data.
  • Camera pictures at the mirror. While the mirror page is open it sends pictures from the camera to our server a few times a second to see who is there. They are checked and thrown away. They are not saved.
  • Brightspace. When you connect Brightspace you sign in to Marist through a browser running on our server. What you type there, including your password and any Duo step, passes through that browser but is not saved by us. We keep the login session it ends with, encrypted, so we can read your courses, calendar, assignments and announcements for your mirror.
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it. Read-only access to the events on your main calendar. We keep an encrypted token that lets us ask Google for your upcoming events. The events themselves are not stored: their titles, times and locations for the next 7 days are fetched when your mirror opens.
  • Your settings. Weather units, where your widgets sit, and a scrambled copy of your API key if you make one.

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

Only to run the mirror: to recognize you and to show your own information to you. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models. The students who run the server can technically reach the database, and only do so to keep the service working.

Information from Google

Sumirrorize's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your calendar events are used only to show them on your mirror. They aren't passed to anyone else, used for advertising, or read by a person.

Where it's kept and who else is involved

  • Everything is kept on a server run by the project team, in a database that can't be reached from the internet. Saved Brightspace logins and Google Calendar tokens are encrypted.
  • Traffic to the site passes through Cloudflare.
  • We talk to Google (sign-in and calendar) and to Marist's Brightspace on your behalf.
  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo and the US National Weather Service. They are told where the mirror is, and nothing about you.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your information until you delete your account or the project ends. On your settings page you can:

  • Disconnect Brightspace, which deletes the saved login.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar, which deletes the token and tells Google to withdraw the access. You can also remove it yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete my account, which erases your face pictures and fingerprints, Brightspace login, calendar access and settings.

Who it's for

Sumirrorize is meant for Marist students. It isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
