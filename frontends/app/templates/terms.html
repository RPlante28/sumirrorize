Terms of Service

Last updated November 15, 2026

Sumirrorize is a smart mirror built by students at Marist University for a hackathon. By signing in you agree to these terms. If you don't, please don't use it.

A student project

Sumirrorize isn't run or endorsed by Marist University, D2L (Brightspace) or Google. It's offered as it is, with no promises: it may be wrong, slow or offline, and it may be shut down at any time, in which case everything saved for you is deleted. Don't rely on it for deadlines. Brightspace is the real record of your classes and due dates.

Your accounts

  • Only connect accounts that are yours: your own Google account and your own Marist login.
  • Only sign up with pictures of your own face.
  • Connecting Brightspace means signing in to Marist through a browser on our server, and letting Sumirrorize read your Brightspace information for you. It's up to you to make sure that's allowed by your school's rules.

What shows on the mirror

Once the mirror recognizes you and sees you blink, it shows your information on its screen, where people standing nearby can read it. The blink check stops a photo of you from opening your mirror, but it isn't strong security.

Fair use

Don't try to get at other people's information, break the service, or use it to do anything illegal. We can remove an account that does.

Your information

What we keep and why is in the Privacy Policy. You can disconnect your accounts or delete everything from your settings page whenever you like.

Changes

If these terms change, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
